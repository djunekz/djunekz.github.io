Principles

Seven rules that decide what gets built, and what gets deleted.

  • Prefer deletion over additionEvery feature is something to maintain. The best change is often a removed line.
  • Build for the moment you are not thereTools should keep working, and keep telling the truth, when nobody is watching.
  • If it needs constant attention, it is already brokenAutomation that demands babysitting has only moved the work somewhere else.
  • Tools should feel inevitableThe right tool looks obvious in hindsight, with nothing to learn twice.
  • Interfaces exist to hide complexity, not expose itA good interface answers the question without making you read the machinery.
  • Trust is a system propertyTrust comes from behavior that is predictable under failure, not from promises.
  • Complexity is debt with interestEvery shortcut and extra layer is borrowed time, and the bill grows.
No roadmap. No promises. Only systems that converge. ∎GitHub · Email · Sitemap
