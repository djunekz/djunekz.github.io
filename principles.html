DESIGN RULES / 04

Principles over
ornament.

A small set of constraints that guide what gets built, what gets simplified and what should be removed.

01

Prefer deletion over addition

Every feature creates a maintenance obligation. Remove unnecessary steps before introducing another layer.

↘
02

Build for the moment you are not there

A tool should remain understandable and dependable without someone watching every operation.

↘
03

If it needs constant attention, it is already broken

Automation should remove recurring work, not relocate it into a new dashboard or babysitting routine.

↘
04

Tools should feel inevitable

The best interface makes the right action obvious and keeps the number of decisions low.

↘
05

Interfaces exist to hide complexity, not expose it

Expose the information people need; keep incidental machinery out of the way.

↘
06

Trust is a system property

Trust is earned through verifiable behavior, predictable failure handling and honest output.

↘
07

Complexity is debt with interest

Every abstraction, dependency and special case has a future cost. Spend that complexity deliberately.

↘

THE STANDARD
